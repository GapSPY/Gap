Why the gap exists

NYSE trades 9:30–16:00 New York, five days a week. The tokens on Robinhood Chain trade every minute of every day against USDG in Uniswap v4 pools. When news lands at 8pm on a Friday, the only place the price can move is here. By Monday morning the token is either ahead of the open or behind it — and it closes that gap in the first minutes of trading.

Premium (amber) means the token trades above the last real price. Discount (blue) means below it.

What $GAP does

It’s the key. Hold the gate amount and the terminal opens fully: every name, every column, alerts. No staking, no lockups, no contract — your balance is read straight from the chain.

Where the fees go

Trading fees from Pons pay for the data feeds and a weekly bounty: the widest gap of the week, called first in the community, paid in USDG. Every payout posted with its transaction.

Honest bits

Prices come from Uniswap v4 pools on Robinhood Chain and from public market quotes. They can lag by a minute. Stock tokens are not shares. Not affiliated with Robinhood Markets, Inc. or Pons. Nothing here is advice.
